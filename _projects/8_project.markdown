Much of the research in the past several years has been focused on how humans and AI systems can work better together. These projects have made use of all of the work that I've done to date:
1. Information visualization for maintaining a common situation assessment and understanding human and AI intent.
2. Multi-agent systems for deploying and operating with AI in different geographic or network contexts.
3. Operator physiology for understanding how engaged operators are with decision-making as part of the team.
4. Organization of team structures to support increasing problem solving abilities or increasing production rates, and determining when to change structures to affect a desired outcome.
5. Interactions to ensure human-agent teams are avoiding groupthink or cognitive surrender.
6. Interactions to enable AI agents to adapt to the ethical priorities of their users, making joint activities more in sync.
